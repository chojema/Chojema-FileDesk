## Changes

- Copy and paste files with Ctrl/⌘+C and Ctrl/⌘+V, to and from Explorer or Finder. Ctrl+X cuts on Windows; ⌘+⌥+V moves on macOS
- Ctrl/⌘+D makes a copy in place, and pasting into the same folder also makes a copy
- Favorite folders moved to Ctrl/⌘+Shift+D
- Windows updates keep taskbar pins. Updating to this version may still unpin once
